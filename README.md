# 🛰️ Arcteon Research & Space

**Architecting Sovereign Geospatial Intelligence for the European Region.**

Arcteon Space is a specialized technology lab focused on high-precision geospatial systems, data sovereignty, and the bridge between satellite science and industrial aplication.

---

### 🌾 Featured Product: Freyr
**Freyr** is our flagship precision agriculture platform. It fuses Sentinel-2 optical and Sentinel-1 SAR imagery — enhanced by the TerraMind foundation model — into actionable harvest guidance and full GAEC compliance reporting for European farmers. Built for simplicity, precision, and 100% EU data residency.

* **Status:** Active Development (v2.1)
* **Access:** Proprietary / Private Repository

---

### 🏗️ Engineering Philosophy
* **Sovereignty by Design:** Our stack is hosted exclusively on EU-sovereign infrastructure to eliminate exposure to the US CLOUD Act.
* **Hardened Architecture:** We employ rigorous production sanitization, including the removal of internal metadata and strict API perimeter defense.
* **High-Signal Execution:** We prioritize robust, type-safe systems (Go, Rust, Python & React/TypeScript) that handle complex geospatial computations at scale.
